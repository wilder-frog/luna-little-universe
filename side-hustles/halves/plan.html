CH 01

Product summary

A website that sells short, emotional reports about love. People take a test or upload a chat. They see part of the result free. They pay a small amount to see all of it.

Market
USA, UK, EU. English only at start.
Buyer
Age 18–34. Uses Instagram and TikTok. In a relationship, or single and asking why.
Price
$3.99 – $6.99. One time. No subscription.
Build time
5 work days with Claude Code.
Fixed cost
About $1–3 per month (domain only). Everything else is free tier or per sale.
AI cost
$0 at launch. Reports come from rules and written text blocks.
Main edge
The two-part couple test. The partner gets questions made from the first person's answers.

CH 03

Couple test flow (P1)

In the examples, Alex starts the test and Sam is the partner.

WARNING

Show the price to Alex before Alex starts. Put one small line under the start button: "Free to take. Full report $6.99 when you both finish."

A price hidden until the end is a deceptive practice under US FTC rules ("drip pricing") and EU consumer law. It also brings chargebacks. Chargebacks close payment accounts.

The pressure still works. Sam spent 6 minutes. Sam left a message. Alex wants to read it. Alex pays for that, not because of a trick.

Tell Sam where Sam's answers go. Sam does not need to see a price. Sam must tick: "I am 18 or older. My answers go into one report that Alex and I can both see."

CH 04

How reports are made (no AI)

Rules + a bank of written text blocks + the person's own answers quoted back. Same answers give the same report. Cost per report: $0.

4.1 Six dimensions

CodeDimensionAlex question (example)Sam mirror question
D1Feeling heard"When we argue, I feel heard." (1–5)"When you argue, how heard do you think Alex feels?" (1–5)
D2Feeling loved"What does Sam need most from you now?" (pick 1 of 6)"What do you need most from Alex now?" (same 6)
D3Safety and trust"I feel safe telling Sam when I am hurt." (1–5)"Alex feels safe telling you when they are hurt." (1–5)
D4Future"Where are we in 3 years?" (pick 1 of 5)Same question.
D5Effort balance"Who tries harder?" (me / Sam / same)Same question, names swapped.
D6Holding back"How often do you hold something back to avoid a fight?""How often do you think Alex holds back?" + Sam's own answer.

The 6 "need" choices for D2: more time together · words said out loud · help with life · touch · space · to feel chosen.

CAUTION

Do not use the name "5 Love Languages" or copy its quiz. It is a registered trademark and the publisher forbids copies.

Do not copy research scales (for example ECR-R) word for word. Commercial use is not confirmed. Use the public ideas (attachment, conflict, effort) and write your own questions.

4.2 Question types

TypeWhoUsed for
Self scale (1–5)BothDimension scores
GuessAlex guesses Sam's answer"How well Alex knows Sam" %
MirrorSam rates Alex's sidePerception gaps
Choice (1 of 5–6)BothMatch / no match
Deep (3 questions)Sam onlyPicked from Alex's lowest dimension. This makes part 2 "built from" part 1.
Message (1 sentence)Both, optionalShown as written. No analysis.

4.3 Scoring procedure

  1. Convert each 1–5 answer to 0–100: (answer − 1) × 25. Flip reverse questions: 100 − score.
  2. Dimension score per person = average of its questions.
  3. Gap per dimension = |Alex self − Sam's mirror of Alex|. Do the same the other way.
  4. Guess accuracy = correct guesses ÷ all guesses × 100.
  5. Sync score = 0.6 × (100 − average difference between Alex and Sam on D1–D6) + 0.4 × guess accuracy. Round to a whole number.
  6. Band each dimension: Low < 40 · Mid 40–69 · High ≥ 70.
  7. Pattern per dimension: both high · both low · Alex higher · Sam higher (difference ≥ 25 counts as "higher").
  8. Pick the couple type from 4.5.

CH 06

Chat report (P3)

The phone reads the chat. Only numbers go to the server. The server sends back the report text. The page adds example lines from the chat on the phone.

Your chat never leaves your phone.

6.1 User steps: export a WhatsApp chat

iPhone
  1. Open the chat.
  2. Tap the contact name.
  3. Tap Export Chat → Without Media.
  4. Save to Files. Pick the file on Halves.
Android
  1. Open the chat.
  2. Tap ⋮ → More → Export chat.
  3. Tap Without media.
  4. Save the file. Pick it on Halves.
CAUTION

  • Group chat: ask the user to pick 2 names.

9.4 Security rules

  • Build the full report text on the server only. The browser never gets paid text before payment.
  • Tokens: 128-bit random. One for A, one for B. Never put names in URLs.
  • Check the webhook signature on every call.
  • Delete test data 60 days after creation. Say so in the privacy page.
  • Rate-limit test creation per IP.
