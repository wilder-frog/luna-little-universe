Scoring rules
# 1. each answer to 0–100
score = (answer − 1) × 25  (flip reverse items: 100 − score)
# 2. dimension = average of its items
# 3. perception gap
gap = | Alex self − Sam's mirror of Alex |
# 4. how well you know them
guess % = right guesses ÷ all guesses × 100
# 5. sync score
sync = 0.6 × (100 − avg difference D1–D6)
       + 0.4 × guess %
# 6. bands
Low < 40 · Mid 40–69 · High ≥ 70
# 7. pattern (difference ≥ 25 = "higher")
both high · both low · Alex higher · Sam higher

Do not use the name "5 Love Languages" or copy its quiz. It is a registered trademark. The publisher forbids copies.

Do not copy research scales (for example ECR-R) word for word. Write your own questions from the public ideas.

Security rules

  • The server builds the full report. The browser never gets paid text before payment.
  • Random 128-bit tokens, one for each person. No names in URLs.
  • Check the webhook signature every time.
  • Delete test data after 60 days.
